9 October — St Dionysius and his Companions, Martyrs; St Louis Bertrand.

Saint Dionysius brings the faith to Gaul and suffers martyrdom with Rusticus and Eleutherius. Saint Louis Bertrand serves as a Dominican, missionary and defender of the oppressed. The complete Butler reading, followed by the day's Reflection.

Source: Alban Butler, Lives of the Saints, Benziger Brothers, 1894 (public domain). Following Butler's traditional calendar.
